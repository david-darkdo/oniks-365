# Project Context - ONIKS365 Digital Showroom

## Company Vision & Mission
ONIKS365 (ONIKS 365 LUXURY KITCHEN AND BATHROOMS FITTINGS) is a proudly Nigerian, CAC-registered company. The vision of the ONIKS365 Digital Showroom is to redefine how premium modern kitchen solutions and luxury bathroom fittings—including WCs, showers, taps, wash basins, water heaters, kitchen sinks, built-in ovens, microwaves, cookers, range hoods, and pantry systems—are discovered, customized, and acquired across Nigeria. Rather than relying on static catalogs, this platform acts as an interactive, digital-first virtual showroom.

## Product & Service Philosophy
*   **Modern Lifestyle Solutions**: Oniks365 provides complete lifestyle solutions combining luxury, durability, modern design, and functionality.
*   **Nationwide Service & Delivery**: Delivering nationwide across Nigeria with payment on delivery available in Abuja and Lagos.
*   **Showrooms & Offices**:
    *   **Abuja Showroom**: 69/243 Cornershop International Building Materials Market, Dei-Dei, Abuja FCT, Nigeria
    *   **Lagos Office**: Odunade Building Materials Market, Coker, Orile, Badagry Expressway, Lagos, Nigeria
*   **WhatsApp Consultation**: Checkout is powered by direct WhatsApp Lookbook Consultation pathways for tailored quotes and architectural guidance.

## Showroom & AI Philosophy
*   **Dynamic Visualizations**: Using Google Imagen 3, the platform automatically generates studio packshots and situated context installations for catalog items.
*   **PIM-Driven Automation**: Uploading a single specification sheet initiates an automated pipeline that drafts luxury marketing copy, designs SEO parameters, writes FAQs, generates professional renderings, and indexes terms.

## Customer Personas & Journey
*   **Premium Architects & Designers**: Use the showroom to discover textures, copy product codes, download brochures, and build client lookbooks.
*   **Luxury Homeowners & Builders**: Browse visual layouts, save curated collections, and request direct consultations for new builds, renovations, or kitchen/bathroom upgrades.

## Core Workflows
1.  **Admin Ingestion**: Admin uploads spec sheet -> system assigns unique sequential product code -> pipeline enqueues AI tasks.
2.  **AI Pipeline**: Analysis of specifications -> generation of descriptions -> generation of studio & context images -> full text search indexing.
3.  **Discovery & Inquiry**: User filters feed -> views details -> adds to a Collection -> requests quote via pre-filled WhatsApp lead message.
